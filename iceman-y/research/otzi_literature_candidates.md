Search date: **2026-10-27 (Europe/Helsinki)**. Inspected baseline: **43273c02ce4a0bd788118d4da6c2a46460894d29**, master, committed 2026-07-27. This is a literature/archive-metadata assessment, not a new genotype analysis, registration or placement. ## Decision

Four individuals warrant a bounded feasibility check: **XAN027**, **SEC002 = I18968**, **SP193**, and **I13517**. None is established here as an independent intermediate Y lineage or as carrying the Z6499-derived/L166-ancestral discriminator. The best first checks are the XAN027 Y-capture target manifest and the SEC002/I18968 release/alias record. SP193 offers a particularly useful pre-alignment shotgun input, but only 0.126× published nuclear coverage. I13517 is a useful possible Z6494-branch comparator with an unusually important upstream damage-selection restriction.

These are **new to the inspected project assessment**, not four newly published discoveries. The relevant papers appeared in 2020–2026; SEC002 has additional capture data released in March 2026. I found no verified post-2026-07-27 human Y-sequence release that changes the project's verdict. The post-baseline Ötzi correction located concerns a viral figure. A May 2026 maternal-tree preprint is also relevant to an up-to-date account of Ötzi, but provides no paternal candidate-selection criterion.

## What the primary literature supports about Ötzi

**Paternal.** Keller et al. 2012 reported the lineage defined by M201, P287, P15, L223 and L91, conventionally G-L91 [S1]. Wang et al. 2023 supplied the much less contaminated 15.3× genome, not a new proof of every downstream Y marker [S2]. Croze et al. 2025 use G-Z6208 / G2a2a1a2a1a1b [S3]. The repository's read-level L166 placement, derived Z6208, ancestral Z6499 and provisional intermediate Z6219 node are separate evidence layers. The live YFull G-L166 page retrieved on the search date still groups Z6219, Z6208, L166, L167 and Z6499/FGC5712 in the same 32-group equivalence block, with Z6494 below it [S4]. This tree grouping does not independently establish the repository's proposed order. No conflicting primary result requiring reopening Ötzi's established genotype finding was identified.

**Maternal.** The complete mitogenome EU810403 supports **K1f**, earlier K1ö/K1*, with the characteristic substitutions at 3513 and 8137; Coia et al. 2016 found this lineage absent or rare in their sampled present-day populations [S5]. Extinction was an inference, not a directly measured SNP state. The **Mitotree preprint**, posted 2026-05-29, reports a living K1f connection and additional ancient branches [S6]. This is a substantive newer qualification to older extinction narratives, not a reason to rename the Y lineage or rank those individuals for Y analysis. The authors' private modern sequences are not publicly available for independent rechecking; their finer maternal topology is reported as a preprint claim here. No migration argument is inferred from either uniparental lineage.

Do not use the existing recovery simulation to certify a Y-only pipeline.

### 3. SP193: useful shotgun input, modest expected power

Source: Arzelier et al. 2022, **10.1016/j.isci.2022.105387**, Tables S1, S2, S6 and S7 [S11]. Table S6 explicitly associates SP193 with **G-PF3239/etc*(xFGC5672^,Z6805,Z6818)**; it associates SP249 with H, resolving the ambiguity of the main text's list order. The FGC5672 exclusion is a published call, with no per-read validation here; the caret is retained, not silently interpreted.

Study **PRJEB50995**, sample **SAMEA13025241 / ERS10623961**, experiment **ERX8283332**, run **ERR8617349**. Submitted **SP193_S23_R1_001.fastq.gz / SP193_S23_R2_001.fastq.gz**, alongside archive-standard paired FASTQs. ENA's **59,685,648 reads** matches Table S2's raw input count; paired NextSeq shotgun and that match strongly support pre-alignment deposition rather than FASTQ reconstructed from a human-only BAM. This is the strongest unfiltered-shotgun provenance evidence among the ranked candidates; it does not prove absence of every prior instrument/quality operation.

Table S2's **shotgun** header gives **0.126× nuclear coverage**, **16.473% quality-filtered endogenous**, mean fragment length **67.85 bp**. These are not Y depth. Table S6's Yleaf output counts are not a measured L166/Z6499 depth either. Published tests of kinship do not demonstrate an independent paternal branch relative to the Swiss men. This sample predates those men but could still share their older patriline. No committed screen or rejection was located. **Recommendation:** a low-cost depth-only gate first; defer an eight-novel-site shared-branch investigation unless enough sites actually have power. This specimen can test the intermediate node even if it proves too shallow for a novel branch.

### 4. I13517: a damage-selected comparator, one paternal unit with I13510

Source: Lazaridis et al. 2022, **10.1126/science.abm4247**, primary Data S1/S2 and supplement pp. 135–136 [S12]. Data S1 assigns **G-Z6494**, automatically using YFull 8.09; it does not supply an independently inspected Z6499 allele. The deeper label is a published assignment, not confirmation of each defining marker. The published contextual date is **1360–1295 BCE**; prose says LH IIIA2, while the metadata's period field says LH IIA2. Retain the numeric range and flag this source inconsistency rather than redating the burial.

Study **PRJEB54831**; sample **SAMEA110396397 / ERS12494592**; nuclear experiment **ERX9583008**, run **ERR10043118**: **I13517_d.bam + BAI**, plus generated FASTQ. mt-only **ERR10043902** is separate. The source defines **_d as restricted to damaged sequences**. Thus the available nuclear object is human mapped, capture enriched and damage selected; conversion to FASTQ cannot restore omitted undamaged molecules. Other exact upstream filters await header documentation.

Four partial/USER libraries were used. Data S1 gives **0.332× coverage on autosomal targets, 295,182 target SNPs hit** for the damage-restricted instance; the 31,662,099 deposited reads and 1.455 Gb are not a license to claim high Y depth. Library-specific pre-restriction metrics in Data S2 must not replace the instance's metric. Authentication and the probability of retaining ancestral/derived reads are affected by selection elsewhere on the molecule, even at a transversion target.

**I13510 is a documented first-degree male relative**, and shares the same paternal unit; it is not a fifth independent candidate. Its shallower G-P287 label need not contradict I13517's label. Its nuclear record is **ERR10043117**, sample **SAMEA110396396 / ERS12494591**, 1240K BAM/BAI. Pooling or joint interpretation would need an explicit prospective rule, contamination review and cross-library deduplication; it is not done here. No independence from other G-Z6494 lineages is established. **Recommendation:** resolve selection/callability first; prioritize behind the other three for the Z6499 direction question.

## Additional Ötzi data and recent literature

Sarhan et al., **The Iceman's microbiome**, 2026-06-03, **10.1186/s40168-026-02417-6**, is new to this project's documented assessment, though earlier than the inspected commit [S17]. The primary paper describes newly generated tissue/water shotgun libraries, alongside amplicons and cultured isolates. **PRJEB94382** includes:

- **ERR15682278**, SAMEA120303323/ERS26965310, ERX15086705: **ITIIIAB = Skin_2954**, fascia/skin from coccyx, sampled 2019-04-29; submitted paired Skin_2954.R1/R2 FASTQs; 152,449,702 archive reads, 23.02 Gb.
- **ERR15682279**, SAMEA120303326/ERS26965313, ERX15086706: **IW18**, internal body water; submitted paired Body_water.R1/R2 FASTQs; 25,241,546 reads, 3.81 Gb.

These are shotgun metagenomic FASTQs, not Y-selected data. Submitted FASTQ format and the methods' fastp processing support a pre-analysis input, but do **not** by themselves prove that human reads were retained before deposition. No human-Y coverage or contamination estimate suitable for this question was found. The separately deposited same-alias amplicons (e.g. ERR15682263/ERR15682266), cultured yeasts and soil controls must not be mistaken for human shotgun libraries. This is a potential route around an input-read restriction, not evidence that discarded 2023 reads can be recovered, and not independent sampling of Ötzi's patriline.

The 2025-12 bioRxiv HPV preprint became Yazigi et al. 2026, **10.1038/s41598-026-49280-x**, followed by **2026-07-29 correction 10.1038/s41598-026-64433-8** [S18]. The correction replaces an earlier Figure 5 about viral E6/E7 variants. No new human Y evidence or new comparison individual relevant to this lineage follows. Fungal/metagenomic and ancestry-method papers returned by the Ötzi search are not evidence of a new paternal placement. The 2023 nuclear resequencing and 2025 Alpine study remain the relevant direct population-genomic baseline, with the repository's marker qualifications preserved.

## Search scope and unresolved access limitations

Searches combined Ötzi/Otzi/Tyrolean Iceman with genome, Y chromosome and mtDNA; PF3239, L91, L166, Z6219, Z6499 and close-marker terms; and broader French, Sardinian, Balkan/Aegean and 2026 release searches. PubMed was queried through the execution date (2023-01-01–2026-10-27); bioRxiv version metadata was checked for the HPV, Mitotree and Iron Gates preprints. Two web search engines, primary journal pages, supplements, current YFull pages, author-hosted supplementary files and ENA read-run/experiment metadata were consulted. Secondary trees/aggregators were discovery leads only; no secondary deep label was adopted as a confirmed genotype. This is not a rebuilt AADR or all-ancient-DNA catalogue, and does not claim exhaustive discovery of every relevant ancient male.

ENA metadata fields checked included study/sample/experiment/run IDs, aliases, library strategy/selection, submitted formats/URLs, generated FASTQ URLs and read/base counts. Counts are archive metadata, not fresh QC. Binary sequence objects were not fetched. Accessible filenames mean the archive advertises public objects; checksums and complete transfer availability were not tested.

PMC attachment URLs intermittently returned verification HTML. Arzelier tables were recovered through the official PMC cloud files, and Southern Arc Data S1/S2 through the authors' supplement ZIP. The live FTDNA Discover pages were inaccessible, so their deeper candidate labels remain unverified leads. YFull pages were successfully retrieved directly despite browser-search timeouts. The July Iron Gates supplement/run crosswalk remains unavailable as described above. There is no access to private modern comparison data or uncommitted local history. Nothing inaccessible is treated as absent.
